Tamir's decision (05.11.2026): the template carousels that Make posted automatically are stopped.
From now on a daily routine produces 2 carousel **videos** per day (9:16, silent) that Tamir uploads
himself to Instagram with music of his choice. They are delivered in the script bank
(https://claude.ai/artifact/VggtRe3ydFhJpKR4RXkSUj, section "סרטוני קרוסלה").

The 110 queue files that were scheduled from 2026-11-05 19:00 to 2026-12-30 were moved here unchanged.
To bring a day back, move its file back to `social/carousels/queue/` (the Make scenario 6462316 picks
up any file whose name matches the current hour). The rendered images stay in their batch folders.
Topics in these files are listed in `social/tools/TOPICS_USED.md`; the daily routine may reuse a topic
only with a new angle.

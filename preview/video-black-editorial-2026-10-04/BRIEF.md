# BRIEF.md

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

Tamir delegated the creative direction: make Menifa look completely different from the current Mercury homepage and from Anton's demo. He rejected the demo's output (black canvas, liquid blob, pill on the left, zigzag masks). The method stands. The brand, the Hebrew, and the mortgage job win.

Evidence is marked. Everything else is an authored decision.

## 0. The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** שולחן עבודה, שני צדדים, קריאה, שקט לפני פתיחה.
   References from other media, not from sites to clone: a bank statement laid next to the kitchen table. A ledger pulled apart so each line can be read. The moment in a working session when one monthly figure becomes a stack of tracks.
2. **Scroll journey, in sequence.** First the payment they already feel. Then the fact that the file is more than one track. Then the tracks get names. Then what a meeting actually checks. Last, one place to book the call. Insurance is one sentence at the end.
3. **Energy.** Quiet at the open. Pressure while the frame holds. The opening of the bar is the loud moment. The checks are quieter. The close settles.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage.** Written in the curve below, before the score. The one moment: the solid payment bar pulls apart into named tracks, and then the ink side takes the screen.
5. **One thing no site they have seen does.** The monthly payment is a physical bar. Scrolling pulls it apart into the tracks inside it. Not a lens, not a blob, not a recoloured spotlight.
6. **Range.** Dense, on the editorial side of finance. Not brutalist, not playful, not retro, not maximalist, not premium-minimal black. Menifa named the kit: ink `#0E3C5C`, accent `#5BAFD8`, gold `#C4A574` as a hairline only.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct sides of one argument, held together for the whole page. Not a flight through a place. Not hard chapter grounds. Two grounds that stay: cool paper for the payment, ink for the mix.
8. **Assets on hand.** Locked logo `assets/images/menifa-logo-transparent.png`. Photograph `assets/images/tamir-og.jpg` (Tamir, existing site photography). Phone `052-4502821`. WhatsApp `https://wa.me/972524502821`. No generated imagery. No kie.ai spend.

Menifa, תמיר גרמה, mortgage advice for someone in Israel who has a payment or an offer and cannot see what the payment is made of.

## Feeling curve

Written before the score. Emotion, then the thing on screen that causes it.

1. Recognition. Both headlines are readable at once, and Tamir's photograph sits in the paper column like a print, not a card.
2. Unease. The frame holds. Three lines name a single debit and a closed file. The payment bar gets heavier.
3. Clarity. The bar pulls apart. Three tracks wipe open and can be read. This is the peak.
4. Confidence. The four checks sit across the split as sentences, quieter than the opening.
5. Resolve. The divider travels to the edge. The ink column keeps the link, the phone, and one insurance sentence.

No two adjacent acts share a feeling.

## Grammar

**Split stage.**

Why the other seven lost:

- Filmic one-shot. Anton's demo already is this shape: fixed bar, one CTA, full-bleed type, one continuous ground, a carried film. It is also the default the first builds fell into. A mortgage decision is a comparison, not a ride.
- Chaptered editorial. It wants a new ground per chapter and a title page with no media. Tamir asked for one continuous designed ground and for the photography already in the repo. The job is a call, not a magazine feature.
- Live surface. A real calculator would show rates. Rates and approval promises are forbidden. A painted dashboard is banned by the grammar's honesty rule.
- Continuous world. It requires a worldflight through a place. There is no footage, generation is off, and a mortgage file is not a geography.
- Typographic poster. It bans photographic ground. The brief requires Tamir's existing photograph.
- Gallery / catalog. It bans a single opening claim and bans persuasion in the labels. The page has to open on the payment, not on a museum of products. There are no verified specs to label.
- Rhythmic cutlist. It bans pin and wants a pulse of short cuts. The feeling is unease, then clarity. A mortgage is a held decision.

## Signature move

A payment bar that scrolling pulls apart into named tracks: פריים, קבועה לא צמודה, צמוד מדד. No percentages. No client totals. Coded on the page from `--sc-p`, not a kit parameter. The engine file is not edited.

Not a lens. Not a blob. Not a spotlight. Not a tilt value.

No invented statistics, savings, approval promises, rates, testimonials, or client counts. No `<<` `>>`. No `«` `»`. Insurance is one sentence in the close. CTA label is locked.

## References actually opened

- cta.gallery. Principle: one label, placed inside the column that wins, not a detached island.
- 60fps.design. Principle: one named state change (a morph / reveal), built from transform and clip, not a decorative loop.
- component.gallery. Principle: do not hide the mix in an accordion or tabs. Both sides stay up until the collapse.
- supahero.io. Principle refused: a title sitting on a full-bleed photo. The first screen is the split, and the photograph stays in its column.
- loadmo.re. Principle: the phone is its own composition. Top and bottom, not a shrunk desktop.
- styles.refero.design. Principle: tokens, not a borrowed style name. Ink, cyan, cool paper, one real photograph.
- recent.design. Principle: it is a directory of people, not a layout to lift.
